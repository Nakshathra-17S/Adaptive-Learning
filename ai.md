# LearnIQ – AI Usage

## 1. Role of AI in LearnIQ

AI is used in LearnIQ to provide an interactive tutoring experience and support personalized learning.

The core adaptive learning logic is based on the student's learning archetype and diagnostic performance.

## 2. AI Tutor

The AI Tutor allows students to ask questions about the learning topic and receive explanations through an interactive chat interface.

The tutor is designed to help students understand concepts, clarify doubts, and continue learning after the main lesson.

## 3. Personalization

LearnIQ does not use AI to determine the student's learning archetype.

The archetype is determined through a dedicated quiz, while the diagnostic assessment determines the student's learning level.

AI is then used as a supporting component within the personalized learning experience.

## 4. Learning Archetypes

The platform supports four learning archetypes:

* Visualizer
* Explorer
* Challenger
* Storyteller

Each archetype receives learning content presented in a different style.

## 5. Responsible AI Usage

AI-generated explanations may occasionally contain inaccuracies.

Students are encouraged to verify important information using reliable educational resources and teacher guidance.

The AI Tutor is intended to support learning and is not intended to replace teachers.

## 6. Future AI Improvements

Future versions could use AI to:

* Analyze learning patterns
* Generate additional practice questions
* Identify areas where students repeatedly struggle
* Adapt explanations based on previous interactions
* Generate personalized revision plans
* Provide more detailed learning analytics
